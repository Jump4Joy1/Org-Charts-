One-time setup

Do this once, on either device. It’s free and needs no card.

  1. Go to supabase.com, sign up, and create a project. Any name, any region, free plan. Wait for it to finish setting up.
  2. In the project, open Project Settings → API. Copy the Project URL and the anon public key into the two fields above, then tap Save project details. Direct links to the remaining pages will appear.
  3. Tap SQL Editor above, paste the SQL from the button below, press Run. It should say Success.
  4. Come back here, enter your email, and tap Email me a sign-in code. Paste whatever the email gives you, a code or the link.
  5. On your other device: same URL, same key, same email. That is the whole thing.

Editing the email template is optional. The app accepts the stock sign-in link as well as a code.

Your charts live in your own Supabase project — not mine, and not on any server I control. Only your signed-in account can read them. Signing out leaves this device’s charts untouched.
